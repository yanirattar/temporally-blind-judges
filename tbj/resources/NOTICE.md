# Third-party material

`physiq_descriptions.csv` is copied unchanged from
[google-deepmind/physics-IQ-benchmark](https://github.com/google-deepmind/physics-IQ-benchmark)
(`descriptions/descriptions_original.csv`), licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
It lists the 396 Physics-IQ videos with their scene descriptions and categories.

The Physics-IQ videos themselves are not included in this repository. `tbj download`
fetches them from the benchmark's public storage bucket.
