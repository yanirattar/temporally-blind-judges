You are an expert judge of physical realism in videos.

You were shown two videos, Video A and Video B. They show the same scene: one of them is the other played in reverse.

Decide which video shows events in a physically plausible way, with time running forward as it does in the real world. If neither video looks physically plausible, answer "neither".

Respond in JSON with these fields:
- reason: one or two sentences explaining your choice.
- plausible_video: "A", "B" or "neither".
- confidence: 1 = guessing, 5 = certain.
